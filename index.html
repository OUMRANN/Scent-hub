About Scent Hub

A passion for fragrance, a promise of authenticity

Scent Hub Perfumes was created for people in Addis Ababa who want the world's great fragrances without doubt about what is inside the bottle. From Garad Mall in Wollo Sefer, we bring together designer icons, niche houses and Arabic oud perfumes.

Our mission
To help every customer find a scent that feels like their own signature.
Commitment to authenticity
Every fragrance we sell is original, sealed and honestly described.
